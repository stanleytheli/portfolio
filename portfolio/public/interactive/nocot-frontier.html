The METR of No-CoT Multiplication

Does “intelligence” predict it?

No-CoT multiplication capability (y) against each model's Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (x) — the latter measured with reasoning fully on (max effort). All 18 models.

Only a moderate link (r ≈ 0.57 — and much of that is small models being weak at both ends), and the telling part is where the frontier chat models sit: the four highest-intelligence models (AA ≥ 51 — Opus 5, Sol, Terra, Sonnet 5) average 4.14, below the mid-index open models (AA 31–44) at 4.66. The most intelligent models are worse no-CoT multipliers — because their intelligence lives in the reasoning we switched off. DeepSeek Pro and the Qwen open models sit well above the trend; the whole Anthropic/OpenAI cluster sits below it.
