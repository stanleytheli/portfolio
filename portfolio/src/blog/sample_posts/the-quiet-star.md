---
title: The Quiet Star
date: 2026-06-15
group: Fiction
summary: A very short story about a star that forgot how to burn.
---

There was once a star that forgot how to burn.

It hung in the far corner of a galaxy no one had bothered to name, dimming
year by year, until even the comets stopped visiting. The star did not mind at
first. Silence, it decided, was a kind of light too.

But one night a small, cold rock drifted close and asked, *"Why don't you
shine anymore?"*

The star thought about this for a thousand years — which, for a star, is about
the length of a held breath.

*"I was waiting,"* it finally said, *"for someone to ask."*

And it began, very slowly, to remember.
